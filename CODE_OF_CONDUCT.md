# Code of conduct

This project is meant to be a place where people can ask questions, report problems and propose changes without it turning into a fight.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Be specific and patient when you review or answer. Not everyone has the context you have.
- Take feedback on your own work without making it personal.
- Keep discussion on topic: the project, its code and its users.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or jokes, or targeting someone for who they are.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of a thread or repeated unwanted contact.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainer, danolez1, through GitHub (a private message or a draft security advisory on this repository both work). Reports are handled privately, and we will not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith.
